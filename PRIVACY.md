# IdeaScout Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

IdeaScout ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version
- **We have no server.** We don't collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no advertising.**
- Your settings, history and saved ideas stay **on your devices**, and sync between your iPhone and iPad through **your own iCloud account**. We can't see them. API keys stay on each device, in its Keychain, and never sync.
- If you choose a **cloud AI provider**, the app sends certain information **directly from your iPhone to that provider**, using **your own API key**, and only after you agree.

## Information sent to the AI provider you choose
To produce your daily ideas, when you use a cloud AI provider (for example Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, Google Gemini, xAI Grok, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, Z.ai GLM, Moonshot Kimi or Xiaomi MiMo) the app sends:
- The text you enter in **About me**, **Interest area** and **My pain points**
- **Public posts and listings** the app collected (for example Hacker News posts and comments, App Store reviews, WordPress plugin reviews and government contract notices)
- Your **notes on a saved project**, when you ask for variant suggestions
- The **details of an idea**, when you ask for its starter kit or ask the app to improve it

The app shows which company receives this, and the country where it's based, and asks for your agreement before sending anything. You can withdraw agreement at any time in Settings → Advanced → AI & costs; the app then uses Apple's on-device AI instead. Some providers are based in China; the app tells you when that's the case.

What the provider does with this information is governed by **its own privacy policy and terms**, under your account with that provider. We recommend reading them.

If you choose **Apple (on-device)**, nothing is sent to any AI provider: processing happens on your device.

## Syncing between your devices
If you're signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your scans, ideas, saved projects, your About me, interest area, pain points and app choices, and a record of what your AI use cost, in **your own private iCloud storage**, so they're the same on your iPhone and iPad. This is stored by Apple in your account under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. Your API keys and the agreements you gave to AI providers are not synced. **Delete all my data** (Settings → About & privacy) also deletes the copies in iCloud and on your other devices. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on the device.

## Information the app fetches
The app downloads public information from services such as Hacker News (via Algolia), GitHub, Apple's App Store feeds, WordPress.org (plugin lists and reviews), AusTender, the Atlassian Marketplace and Hugging Face. These requests contain no personal information beyond what any internet request contains (such as your IP address, seen by that service). The one exception is your **interest area**: it's sent to Hacker News's search (Algolia) to find posts about it. The other sources only use it on your device, to pick what to read first.

## Purchases
IdeaScout is free. The optional tips ("Shout me a coffee") are handled entirely by **Apple** through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details or learn who tipped.

## Children
The app isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
