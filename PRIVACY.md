# IdeaScout Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

IdeaScout ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version
- **We have no server.** We don't collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no advertising.**
- Your settings, history, saved ideas and API keys stay **on your iPhone**. API keys are kept in the iPhone Keychain.
- If you choose a **cloud AI provider**, the app sends certain information **directly from your iPhone to that provider**, using **your own API key**, and only after you agree.

## Information sent to the AI provider you choose
To produce your daily ideas, when you use a cloud AI provider (for example Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, Google Gemini, xAI Grok, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, Z.ai GLM, Moonshot Kimi or Xiaomi MiMo) the app sends:
- The text you enter in **About me**, **Industry focus** and **My Business Challenges**
- **Public posts and listings** the app collected (for example Hacker News posts and comments, App Store reviews, government contract notices, startup listings)
- Your **notes on a saved project**, when you ask for variant suggestions
- The **details of an idea**, when you ask for its starter kit or ask the app to improve it

The app shows which company receives this, and the country where it's based, and asks for your agreement before sending anything. You can withdraw agreement at any time in Settings → Advanced → AI & costs; the app then uses Apple's on-device AI instead. Some providers are based in China; the app tells you when that's the case.

What the provider does with this information is governed by **its own privacy policy and terms**, under your account with that provider. We recommend reading them.

If you choose **Apple (on-device)**, nothing is sent to any AI provider: processing happens on your iPhone.

## Information the app fetches
The app downloads public information from services such as Hacker News (via Algolia), GitHub, Apple's App Store feeds, AusTender, the Atlassian Marketplace, Y Combinator company data (via the yc-oss project) and Hugging Face. These requests contain no personal information beyond what any internet request contains (such as your IP address, seen by that service).

## Purchases
The free trial and the one-off unlock are handled by **Apple** through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

## Children
The app isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
